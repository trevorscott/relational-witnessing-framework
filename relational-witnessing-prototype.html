An interactive framework

The universe may be timeless. So why does now feel real?

Imagine the universe not as a movie playing frame by frame, but as the entire reel existing at once. This framework asks how a point of view—and a sense of time—could still arise inside it.

Orientation

The whole can be still while relationships inside it tell a story.

Draw a boundary around one part of the universe, and three things appear together: a point of view, incomplete information, and a way to order change.
Scope matters. This is a proposed way to connect existing ideas—not a claim that consciousness has been solved. It clarifies what the physics can explain, what requires extra assumptions, and exactly where the mystery remains.

Starting constraint. The global state satisfies Ĥ|Ψ⟩ = 0 and is pure, so S(ρtotal) = 0. “Timeless” here means no evolution with respect to an external coordinate parameter.
Interpretive boundary. The partial trace theorem fixes all local observable statistics. Reading that reduced state as the witness’s epistemic perspective is explicitly interpretive; the formal construction does not require wave-function collapse.

Make one imaginary cut. A point of view appears.

This is not a physical slice through space. It means choosing one part of the universe and asking: what could the world look like from only that part?

The key idea

The witness is uncertain not because the universe itself is incomplete, but because part of the information belongs to its relationship with everything else.

The two canonical maps. The bipartition Htotal = HW ⊗ HR induces (1) ρW = TrR(|Ψ⟩⟨Ψ|), which fixes the local statistics and entanglement entropy, and (2) |ψ̃(τ)⟩ = (⟨w(τ)|C ⊗ 𝟙)|Ψ⟩, which defines a family of states conditional on internal clock readings.
The factorization objection. Tensor-product structure is not unique a priori. The paper selects the factorization through the physical meaning and locality of a specified observable algebra, with non-interaction as a supplementary condition on the clock factor. Whether this fully fixes the memory and internal factorizations remains the framework’s most dangerous open issue.

02 · Time lab

Time appears when a clock and a memory can be compared.

From outside, the whole pattern is unchanged. From inside, one part can act as a clock while another stores records. Comparing “the record when the clock says this” with “the record when the clock says that” creates an internal order.

In ordinary language

No remembered difference, no meaningful “before” and “after.” In this model, time is the order found in changing records—not a universal river flowing outside the universe.

Clock–memory decomposition. HW = HC ⊗ HM. The clock C remains non-interacting for Page–Wootters consistency; the memory M may interact with R and bears the information cost.
Do not overread the result. Directly inserting Landauer’s lower energy bound into the denominator of Margolus–Levitin would be invalid. The displayed relation follows only after assuming equality at the Landauer limit and a shared energy budget between clock orthogonalization and memory overwrite.

03 · Entanglement topology

Being connected is not the same as being conscious.

A tiny quantum system can be connected to its surroundings without having an experience. The framework therefore asks whether the shape and complexity of connections inside a witness could distinguish different kinds of conscious state.

How to read the picture

Dots are parts of the witness. Lines are strong quantum connections. Loops and filled shapes show organization that survives as weaker connections are ignored.

Flag complex. Internal subsystems become vertices; pairwise logarithmic negativities wij become weighted edges. A higher simplex appears exactly when all of its pairwise edges appear, with weight equal to the weakest edge.
Known limitation. A flag complex built from pair marginals cannot detect genuinely multipartite entanglement with vanishing pairwise negativity, such as GHZ-type structure. The visualization is therefore a model of pairwise-negativity topology, not total quantum integration.

04 · Research map

What is established, what is proposed, and what is still unknown?

The framework is deliberately honest about its boundaries. Some ingredients come from established physics. Some connections require assumptions. The consciousness bridge and topological hypothesis remain open research.

Established ingredients

Quantum theory allows a globally fixed state, local reduced viewpoints, internal clocks, and physical limits connecting information, energy, and distinguishable change.

The framework’s synthesis

A single boundary can co-generate the local viewpoint, its uncertainty, and its relational ordering of records.

Assumed for the time bound

The witness is modeled as maximally energy-efficient, using a shared energy budget, while its memory records change monotonically.

Still unsolved

Why any physical structure produces felt experience, whether connection topology is the right bridge, and whether the preferred boundary is uniquely fixed.
